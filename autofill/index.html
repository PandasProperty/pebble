How it works

1

Create your profiles

Add a profile for each identity you fill in: personal, work, a QA test persona. Each holds name, email, phone, company, address and any extra fields a form asks for. Profiles are stored in your browser and follow you through your own Chrome sync.

2

Fill a form three ways

Click the toolbar button and press Fill this page, right-click and choose Fill form with Work (or any other profile), or press Alt+Shift+F to fill with your default profile.

3

It finds the fields other fillers miss

Fields are recognised by name, id, autocomplete, label, placeholder and aria-label, with built-in synonyms, so "fname", "first_name" and "given-name" all count as first name. It fills text boxes, dropdowns, checkboxes and radio buttons, picks "United Kingdom" for "UK", and works with React and Vue forms. Rules cover anything left over.

Questions

Is it safe? What data leaves my browser?

Nothing about you. Profiles, rules and settings stay in Chrome's storage, and form contents are never sent anywhere. There is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only network request is to extensionpay.com, with an anonymous random identifier, to check whether Pro has been bought. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why does it need these permissions?
PermissionWhy
activeTabTemporary access to the current tab, only when you click the toolbar button, a right-click entry or the shortcut, so it can fill that page. No access to any other page.
scriptingRuns the filling script in that tab after you trigger a fill, and on the sites you add for Pro auto-fill.
contextMenusAdds "Fill form with <profile>" to the right-click menu.
storageSaves your profiles, rules and settings, and remembers whether you bought Pro.
site accessNever granted at install. Requested for one site at a time, only when you add it to auto-fill on page load. Removing the site revokes it.
extensionpay.comPart of the payment library: on ExtensionPay's own checkout page it learns that a purchase completed. It reads nothing else.
Does it fill passwords or card numbers?

Never. It skips password, one-time code, CAPTCHA, card number and security code fields, even if a profile or rule would match them.

What does the free version include?

Unlimited fills with no daily limit, two profiles, keyword rules, and all three ways to fill: toolbar button, right-click menu and keyboard shortcut. It does not expire.

Pro is a single payment of US$12.99, with no subscription and no renewal. Checkout happens on ExtensionPay's and Stripe's pages; the extension never sees your card.

How do I restore Pro on another computer?

Install Pebble Autofill there, open its settings and choose Restore purchase, then use the email address you paid with.

Does it work on Microsoft Edge?

Yes. Edge and other Chromium-based browsers can install it from the Chrome Web Store; in Edge, allow extensions from other stores when it asks. A listing on Edge Add-ons is coming.
