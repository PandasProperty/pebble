Privacy policy: Pebble Auto Refresh

Effective 2 November 2026 · All Pebble extensions

Pebble Auto Refresh does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Timers, site rules and settings stay in your browser. The extension has no analytics and no tracking.

What the extension stores

Active timers, your site rules and your settings are stored only in your browser, using Chrome's extension storage. Running timers are kept in session storage and are cleared when Chrome closes. The extension also stores a flag saying whether Pro has been purchased, and a random identifier created by ExtensionPay on install (see Purchases below).

What the extension sends

Nothing about you. The extension has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. It does not record which pages you refresh. The only network requests it makes are to extensionpay.com, to check whether Pro has been bought.

When the extension reads a page

Refreshing a tab needs no access to the page. Two Pro options do read the page, and only on tabs where you turn them on: Pause while typing checks whether a form field is focused before a reload, and Stop when the page contains looks for the text you entered after each reload. Chrome asks you to allow the site when you enable either option or add a site rule. The text of the page is examined in your browser and never leaves it.

Purchases

Pro is sold by Anda Tech Solutions SRL, the developer, not by Google. To check whether you purchased Pro, the extension contacts ExtensionPay using an anonymous random identifier created on install. If you buy Pro, checkout happens on ExtensionPay's and Stripe's pages, which collect the email address and payment details needed to process and restore the purchase, under their own privacy policies (ExtensionPay, and Stripe). The extension itself never sees your card details.

Your data, your control

You can edit or delete site rules at any time from the extension's settings, stop any timer from the popup, and remove everything by uninstalling the extension. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Changes that affect how data is handled will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
